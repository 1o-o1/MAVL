LIMITATIONS & CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. SMALL TEST SET (668 images)
   Memory module benefits may not manifest on small datasets. Revalidation
   on MIMIC-CXR (40K+ test) and VinDr-CXR (18K test) recommended.

2. CHEXPERT LABEL QUALITY
   Labels via NLP extraction, not manual radiologist review.
   Clinical deployment requires additional validation.

3. METADATA LIMITATIONS
   CheXpert public test split lacks sex, age, body region labels.
   Fairness analysis limited to view (AP/PA/LAT only).

4. EXPLAINABILITY QUALITATIVE ONLY
   Grad-CAM lacks ground-truth bounding-box validation on CheXpert.
   Quantitative IoU assessment deferred to VinDr-CXR (18K with radiologist boxes).

5. TEMPERATURE SCALING ON SMALL VALIDATION SET
   T learned on ~100 validation images. Uncertainty estimate recommended.

6. MULTI-LABEL STRUCTURE
   CheXpert images have 2-4 pathologies per image (cumulative prevalence >100%).
   Single-threshold optimization may not capture multi-label complexity.
